## Difficulty Scale Definition

| Level | Label | Description | Example |
|-------|-------|------------|---------|
| 1 | Basic | Direct recall of a definition, formula, or fact | "What is the SI unit of amount of substance?" |
| 2 | Standard | Apply a single concept or formula directly | "Calculate the number of moles in 36 g of water" |
| 3 | Moderate | Multi-step problem requiring 2-3 connected concepts | "Using the ideal gas law, calculate the volume of CO2 produced from 10g of CaCO3 at STP" |
| 4 | Advanced | Complex analysis requiring synthesis of multiple concepts or error identification | "Explain why the experimental molar mass of acetic acid in benzene is approximately 120 g/mol instead of 60 g/mol" |
| 5 | Expert | Evaluation, design, or novel problem requiring deep understanding | "Design an experiment to determine the order of reaction for the decomposition of H2O2, including apparatus, procedure, and how you would analyze the data" |

## Bloom's Taxonomy Level Mapping

| Level | Verb | Chemistry Context |
|-------|------|------------------|
| Remember | Define, list, state, name, recall | State the Aufbau principle; Name the functional group |
| Understand | Explain, describe, compare, classify, interpret | Explain why ionic compounds conduct when molten; Compare SN1 and SN2 mechanisms |
| Apply | Calculate, solve, use, determine, predict | Calculate pH from Ka; Predict the product of an addition reaction |
| Analyze | Distinguish, identify (cause), relate, deduce | Deduce the structure from IR and NMR data; Analyze why a reaction is not at equilibrium |
| Evaluate | Justify, assess, critique, evaluate, judge | Evaluate which catalyst is more suitable; Justify the choice of indicator for a titration |
| Create | Design, propose, plan, construct, formulate | Design a synthesis route for aspirin; Propose an experiment to test a hypothesis |
