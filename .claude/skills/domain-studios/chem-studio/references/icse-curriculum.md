# ISC Chemistry Curriculum Reference

## ISC Class 11 Chemistry

### Unit 1: Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry
- General introduction: importance and scope of chemistry
- Nature of matter: states of matter, classification of matter (element, compound, mixture)
- Laws of chemical combination: law of conservation of mass, law of definite proportions, law of multiple proportions, Gay-Lussac's law, Avogadro's law
- Dalton's atomic theory
- Mole concept and molar mass, Avogadro's number
- Percentage composition, empirical and molecular formulae
- Chemical equations and stoichiometry, limiting reagent concept

### Unit 2: Structure of Atom
- Subatomic particles: electron, proton, neutron -- discovery and properties
- Atomic models: Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr
- Quantum mechanical model: dual nature of matter and radiation, de Broglie equation
- Heisenberg uncertainty principle
- Quantum numbers (principal, angular momentum, magnetic, spin)
- Shapes of orbitals (s, p, d)
- Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion principle, Hund's rule of maximum multiplicity
- Electronic configuration of elements (up to Z=30)
- Stability of half-filled and fully filled orbitals
- Concept of atomic spectra: emission and absorption

### Unit 3: Classification of Elements and Periodicity in Properties
- Need for classification, early attempts (Dobereiner's triads, Newlands' octaves)
- Mendeleev's periodic table and its limitations
- Modern periodic table based on electronic configuration
- Types of elements: s-block, p-block, d-block, f-block
- Periodic properties: ionization enthalpy, electron gain enthalpy, electronegativity (Pauling scale), atomic and ionic radii, metallic and non-metallic character
- Periodicity of valency and oxidation states

### Unit 4: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure
- Kossel-Lewis approach to chemical bonding
- Ionic bond: formation, lattice energy (Born-Haber cycle), properties of ionic compounds
- Covalent bond: Lewis structure, formal charge, limitations of octet rule
- Bond characteristics: bond length, bond angle, bond enthalpy, bond order, resonance
- VSEPR theory: predicting shapes of molecules
- Valence Bond Theory: orbital overlap, types of overlap (sigma, pi), hybridization (sp, sp2, sp3, sp3d, sp3d2)
- Molecular Orbital Theory: LCAO, bonding and antibonding MOs, bond order, electronic configuration of homonuclear diatomics (H2 through Ne2), paramagnetic and diamagnetic behavior
- Hydrogen bonding: types and effects on physical properties

### Unit 5: States of Matter: Gases and Liquids
- Three states of matter
- Intermolecular interactions: types and strengths
- Gas laws: Boyle's law, Charles's law, Gay-Lussac's law, Avogadro's law, ideal gas equation
- Dalton's law of partial pressures, Graham's law of diffusion
- Kinetic molecular theory of gases (postulates, derivation of PV = 1/3 mnc^2)
- Deviation from ideal behavior, van der Waals equation, compressibility factor
- Liquefaction of gases, critical temperature
- Liquid state: vapor pressure, viscosity, surface tension

### Unit 6: Chemical Thermodynamics
- Thermodynamic terms: system, surroundings, types of systems, state variables, state functions, intensive and extensive properties
- First law of thermodynamics: internal energy, work, heat, enthalpy
- Heat capacity: Cp and Cv, relationship between Cp and Cv for ideal gas
- Measurement of enthalpy changes: calorimetry
- Enthalpy changes: formation, combustion, bond dissociation, atomization, phase transition, solution, dilution, lattice energy
- Hess's law and Born-Haber cycle
- Second law of thermodynamics: entropy, spontaneity, Gibbs free energy
- Gibbs-Helmholtz equation: Delta G = Delta H - T Delta S
- Conditions for spontaneity

### Unit 7: Equilibrium
- Equilibrium in physical and chemical processes
- Law of chemical equilibrium, equilibrium constant (Kc, Kp), relationship between Kc and Kp
- Factors affecting equilibrium, Le Chatelier's principle
- Ionic equilibrium in solution: Arrhenius, Bronsted-Lowry, and Lewis concepts
- Ionization of acids and bases, ionization constant (Ka, Kb), ionic product of water (Kw)
- pH scale, buffer solutions (Henderson-Hasselbalch equation)
- Solubility equilibrium: solubility product (Ksp), common ion effect

### Unit 8: Redox Reactions
- Classical and electronic concepts of oxidation and reduction
- Oxidation number: rules and calculation
- Balancing redox equations: oxidation number method, half-reaction (ion-electron) method
- Types of redox reactions: combination, decomposition, displacement, disproportionation
- Applications in electrode processes

### Unit 9: Hydrogen
- Position in periodic table, occurrence, isotopes
- Preparation of dihydrogen: laboratory and industrial methods
- Properties: physical and chemical
- Hydrides: ionic, covalent, interstitial
- Water: structure, properties, heavy water
- Hydrogen peroxide: preparation, properties, structure, uses
- Hydrogen as a fuel

### Unit 10: s-Block Elements
- Group 1 (alkali metals) and Group 2 (alkaline earth metals)
- General introduction, electronic configuration, occurrence, trends in physical and chemical properties
- Anomalous properties of first elements (lithium, beryllium)
- Important compounds: NaOH, Na2CO3, NaHCO3, NaCl, CaO, Ca(OH)2, CaCO3, CaSO4, Plaster of Paris, cement
- Biological importance of Na, K, Mg, Ca

### Unit 11: Some p-Block Elements
- Group 13 and Group 14 elements
- General introduction, electronic configuration, occurrence, variation of properties, oxidation states, trends in chemical reactivity
- Anomalous properties of first elements (boron, carbon)
- Important compounds: borax, boric acid, boron hydrides, aluminium (reactions with acids, alkalis), allotropes of carbon, silicon dioxide, silicones, silicates, zeolites

### Unit 12: Organic Chemistry -- Some Basic Principles and Techniques
- General introduction, purification methods (crystallisation, sublimation, distillation, differential extraction, chromatography)
- Qualitative analysis: detection of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, halogens, sulphur, phosphorus
- Quantitative analysis: estimation of carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, halogens, sulphur, phosphorus (calculations)
- Classification and IUPAC nomenclature
- Isomerism: structural (chain, position, functional group, metamerism, tautomerism)
- Electronic displacement effects: inductive, electromeric, resonance, hyperconjugation
- Bond fission: homolytic and heterolytic
- Reactive intermediates: free radicals, carbocations, carbanions, carbenes
- Types of reactions: substitution, addition, elimination, rearrangement

### Unit 13: Hydrocarbons
- Alkanes: nomenclature, isomerism, conformations of ethane and butane, physical properties, reactions (combustion, halogenation with mechanism, Wurtz reaction, controlled oxidation, isomerization, aromatization, pyrolysis)
- Alkenes: nomenclature, structure of double bond, geometrical isomerism, physical properties, preparation, reactions (addition of H2, halogens, HX, H2O; Markovnikov's and anti-Markovnikov's rule; ozonolysis, oxidation, polymerization)
- Alkynes: nomenclature, structure of triple bond, physical properties, preparation, reactions (acidic character, addition reactions, polymerization)
- Aromatic hydrocarbons: benzene (structure, resonance, aromaticity, Huckel rule), reactions (electrophilic substitution: halogenation, nitration, sulphonation, Friedel-Crafts alkylation and acylation), directive effects of substituents, addition reactions of benzene

### Unit 14: Environmental Chemistry
- Environmental pollution: air, water, soil
- Atmospheric pollution: tropospheric and stratospheric
- Gaseous pollutants (SO2, NOx, CO, hydrocarbons), particulate pollutants (smoke, dust, fumes, mist)
- Acid rain, photochemical smog, ozone layer depletion
- Water pollution: causes, international standards for drinking water
- Soil pollution: pesticides, industrial waste
- Green chemistry: principles and applications
- Strategies for control of environmental pollution

## ISC Class 12 Chemistry

### Unit 1: Solid State
- Classification of solids, crystal lattices and unit cells
- Calculation of density, packing efficiency (BCC, FCC, HCP)
- Voids: tetrahedral and octahedral, radius ratio rules
- Imperfections in solids: point defects (stoichiometric and non-stoichiometric), Schottky and Frenkel defects
- Electrical properties: conductors, semiconductors, insulators, band theory, n-type and p-type
- Magnetic properties: diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic, antiferromagnetic, ferrimagnetic

### Unit 2: Solutions
- Types of solutions, molarity, molality, mole fraction, mass percentage
- Solubility: factors affecting, Henry's law
- Vapor pressure of liquid solutions: Raoult's law for volatile and non-volatile solutes
- Ideal and non-ideal solutions: positive and negative deviations, azeotropes
- Colligative properties: RLVP, elevation of boiling point, depression of freezing point, osmotic pressure
- Determination of molecular mass, Van't Hoff factor for electrolyte solutions

### Unit 3: Electrochemistry
- Electrochemical cells: galvanic and electrolytic
- Electrode potential, standard electrode potential, electrochemical series
- Nernst equation, cell potential calculations
- Gibbs energy and cell potential: Delta G = -nFE
- Conductance of electrolytic solutions: specific, molar, equivalent conductivity
- Variation of conductivity with concentration, Kohlrausch's law
- Electrolysis: Faraday's laws, applications
- Batteries: primary (dry cell, mercury cell) and secondary (lead storage, Ni-Cd)
- Fuel cells, corrosion and its prevention

### Unit 4: Chemical Kinetics
- Rate of a chemical reaction: average and instantaneous
- Factors affecting rate: concentration, temperature, nature of reactants, catalyst, surface area
- Rate law, rate constant, order and molecularity
- Integrated rate equations: zero order, first order, pseudo-first order
- Half-life of a reaction
- Temperature dependence: Arrhenius equation, activation energy, collision theory

### Unit 5: Surface Chemistry
- Adsorption: physisorption and chemisorption, adsorption isotherms (Freundlich)
- Catalysis: types, characteristics, activity, selectivity
- Enzyme catalysis: Michaelis-Menten mechanism
- Colloids: classification, preparation, purification, properties (Tyndall effect, Brownian motion, electrophoresis, coagulation), applications
- Emulsions: types, preparation, properties

### Unit 6: General Principles and Processes of Isolation of Elements
- Occurrence of metals, concentration of ores
- Thermodynamic principles: Ellingham diagram
- Extraction: roasting, calcination, smelting, refining
- Extraction of Al, Cu, Zn, Fe with specific details

### Unit 7: p-Block Elements (Groups 15-18)
- Group 15: nitrogen family; ammonia, nitric acid, phosphine, PCl3, PCl5, oxoacids of phosphorus
- Group 16: oxygen family; ozone, SO2, H2SO4 (contact process), oxoacids of sulphur
- Group 17: halogen family; HCl, oxoacids of halogens, interhalogen compounds
- Group 18: noble gases; compounds of xenon (XeF2, XeF4, XeF6, XeOF2, XeO3), uses

### Unit 8: d- and f-Block Elements
- Transition elements: general characteristics, electronic configuration, variable oxidation states, colored ions, complex formation, catalytic properties, interstitial compounds, alloys
- Preparation and properties: KMnO4, K2Cr2O7
- Inner transition elements: lanthanoids (electronic configuration, oxidation states, lanthanoid contraction), actinoids (comparison with lanthanoids)

### Unit 9: Coordination Compounds
- Werner's theory, double salts vs. coordination compounds
- Terminology: central atom, ligands (types), coordination number, coordination sphere
- IUPAC nomenclature, isomerism (geometric, optical, linkage, coordination, ionization, solvate)
- Bonding: VBT, CFT (crystal field splitting in octahedral and tetrahedral fields, CFSE, spectrochemical series)
- Color, magnetic properties, stability (stability constants)
- Applications in biology, medicine, industry

### Unit 10: Haloalkanes and Haloarenes
- Classification, nomenclature, isomerism, preparation, properties
- Mechanisms: SN1, SN2, elimination (E1, E2 -- brief)
- Optical activity, chirality, enantiomers, racemic mixtures
- Haloarenes: electrophilic substitution, nucleophilic substitution (Dow process)
- Polyhalogen compounds: chloroform, carbon tetrachloride, DDT, freons

### Unit 11: Alcohols, Phenols, and Ethers
- Classification, nomenclature, isomerism, preparation, properties
- Alcohols: reactions (with HX, PX3, SOCl2, dehydration, oxidation, esterification), identification tests
- Phenols: acidity, electrophilic substitution, Kolbe's reaction, Reimer-Tiemann, coupling
- Ethers: preparation (Williamson synthesis), properties

### Unit 12: Aldehydes, Ketones, and Carboxylic Acids
- Nomenclature, preparation, properties
- Nucleophilic addition mechanism (carbonyl group)
- Reactions: oxidation, reduction, Tollens', Fehling's, Cannizzaro, aldol condensation, cross-aldol, Clemmensen reduction, Wolff-Kishner reduction
- Carboxylic acids: acidity, Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky reaction, ester hydrolysis, decarboxylation

### Unit 13: Amines
- Classification, nomenclature, preparation (reduction of nitro compounds, ammonolysis, Gabriel synthesis, Hofmann bromamide reaction)
- Properties: basicity (comparison of aliphatic and aromatic amines), chemical reactions
- Diazonium salts: preparation, reactions (Sandmeyer, Gattermann)

### Unit 14: Biomolecules
- Carbohydrates: classification, structures of glucose and fructose, cyclic structures (Haworth), anomers, mutarotation, disaccharides (sucrose), polysaccharides
- Proteins: amino acids (classification, zwitterion), peptide bond, levels of structure (primary through quaternary), denaturation, enzymes
- Nucleic acids: DNA and RNA structure, biological functions, replication and protein synthesis (overview)
- Vitamins and hormones: classification, deficiency diseases

### Unit 15: Polymers
- Classification: based on source, structure, polymerization type, molecular forces
- Addition polymerization: free radical mechanism, copolymerization
- Condensation polymerization: polyester, nylon, Bakelite
- Natural polymers: rubber (vulcanization), cellulose, starch, proteins
- Biodegradable polymers: PHBV, nylon-2-nylon-6

### Unit 16: Chemistry in Everyday Life
- Drugs: classification, drug-target interaction, therapeutic action
- Drug classes: analgesics, tranquillizers, antiseptics, disinfectants, antimicrobials, antifertility, antihistamines, antacids, antibiotics
- Chemicals in food: artificial sweeteners, preservatives, antioxidants
- Soaps and detergents: types, cleansing action, environmental impact
