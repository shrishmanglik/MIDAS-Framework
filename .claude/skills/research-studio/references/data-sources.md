# Trusted Data Sources by Domain

## Government & Regulatory
- **US Census Bureau** — Demographics, economic data. Free. census.gov
- **Bureau of Labor Statistics** — Employment, wages, CPI. Free. bls.gov
- **SEC EDGAR** — Public company filings (10-K, S-1). Free. sec.gov
- **Statistics Canada** — Canadian demographics, economics. Free. statcan.gc.ca
- **World Bank** — Global economic indicators. Free. data.worldbank.org

## Industry Reports (Premium)
- **Gartner** — Technology market analysis, Magic Quadrants
- **Forrester** — Technology, marketing, CX research
- **McKinsey Global Institute** — Economic and business strategy
- **CB Insights** — Startup/VC data, market maps
- **Statista** — Cross-industry statistics aggregator

## Technology
- **GitHub** — Repository metrics, language trends, community activity
- **Stack Overflow Survey** — Developer tool preferences, salary data
- **npm/PyPI** — Package download counts, ecosystem health
- **BuiltWith** — Technology adoption across websites
- **ThoughtWorks Technology Radar** — Emerging tech assessment

## Startup & VC
- **Crunchbase** — Funding rounds, company profiles
- **PitchBook** — VC deal data, valuations
- **AngelList** — Startup job postings, hiring signals
- **Product Hunt** — New product launches, user reception

## Financial
- **SEC EDGAR** — US public company filings
- **SEDAR+** — Canadian public company filings
- **Yahoo Finance / Google Finance** — Stock data, financial summaries
- **Bloomberg Terminal** — Comprehensive financial data (premium)

## Source Credibility Tiers
| Tier | Type | Examples | Trust Level |
|------|------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Government / Regulatory | SEC, Census, BLS | Highest — use without caveat |
| 2 | Peer-reviewed / Academic | Published research papers | High — note methodology |
| 3 | Major analyst firms | Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey | High — note potential bias |
| 4 | Industry associations | Trade groups, professional orgs | Medium — verify methodology |
| 5 | News / Press | Major outlets, tech blogs | Medium — corroborate with Tier 1-3 |
| 6 | Company claims | Press releases, marketing | Low — independent verification required |
