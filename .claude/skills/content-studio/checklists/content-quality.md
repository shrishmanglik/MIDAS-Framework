# Content Quality Checklist

## Description
The universal quality checklist that every piece of content must pass before distribution. Used by the Content Editor during Phase 4 (Edit) and by any agent self-checking their work. This checklist is the gatekeeper between "drafted" and "ready to publish."

## How to Use
Run through every section. Check each item. If ANY item in the "Must Pass" sections fails, the content goes back for revision. Items in "Should Pass" are strong recommendations — failing one doesn't block publication but should be justified.

---

## 1. Brief Alignment (Must Pass)

- [ ] Content matches the stated objective from the content brief
- [ ] Target audience is clearly the intended persona (not too broad, not mismatched)
- [ ] Key messages from the brief are present in the content
- [ ] Format matches what was specified (post, article, thread, email, script)
- [ ] CTA aligns with the brief's goal (not a different ask)
- [ ] Tier classification is correct (didn't use Tier 3 when Tier 1 template sufficed)

## 2. Hook / Opening (Must Pass)

- [ ] First 2 lines (LinkedIn) or first 8 seconds (video) or subject line (email) would stop YOU from scrolling
- [ ] Hook creates curiosity, presents a stat, tells a story, or makes a bold claim — not generic filler
- [ ] Hook works WITHOUT the rest of the content (it's valuable or intriguing standalone)
- [ ] No "I'm excited to share" or "In today's fast-paced world" or other empty openers

## 3. Brand Voice (Must Pass)

- [ ] Content sounds like MDS — smart friend who happens to be an expert
- [ ] No corporate jargon: "leverage," "synergy," "paradigm," "innovative," "disruptive," "value proposition," "thought leader"
- [ ] No desperation: "Don't miss out," "Act now," "You NEED this"
- [ ] No emoji-as-bullet-points (especially the pointing finger emoji)
- [ ] Contractions used naturally ("we're" not "we are" — unless emphasis requires the full form)
- [ ] Technical terms are used accurately but explained when needed
- [ ] Tone is consistent throughout — no jarring shifts from casual to formal

## 4. Clarity and Readability (Must Pass)

- [ ] Every sentence can be understood on first reading
- [ ] Paragraphs are 1-4 sentences (mobile readability)
- [ ] No walls of text — adequate line breaks and white space
- [ ] Technical concepts have analogies or examples for less technical readers
- [ ] Acronyms are defined on first use
- [ ] Jargon is justified — if a simpler word works, use the simpler word
- [ ] Content can be read aloud without stumbling (especially for scripts)

## 5. Structure and Flow (Must Pass)

- [ ] Content has a clear beginning, middle, and end
- [ ] Each section earns its place — no filler sections that repeat earlier points
- [ ] Transitions between sections are smooth (not abrupt topic jumps)
- [ ] Information builds logically — later points depend on earlier ones
- [ ] The content doesn't just end — it concludes with purpose

## 6. Evidence and Accuracy (Must Pass)

- [ ] Every statistic has a source or is clearly labeled as internal data
- [ ] No unverifiable claims presented as facts
- [ ] Numbers are consistent throughout (don't say "$0.003" in one place and "$0.002" in another)
- [ ] Technical claims are accurate (code examples work, architecture descriptions are correct)
- [ ] No outdated information presented as current
- [ ] Competitor references are fair and accurate (no strawman arguments)

## 7. CTA (Must Pass)

- [ ] Single, clear call to action (not three different asks)
- [ ] CTA matches the relationship stage (don't ask cold audiences to book a call; don't ask warm audiences to just "follow")
- [ ] CTA is specific: "DM me 'architecture'" is better than "reach out"
- [ ] CTA is placed at a natural conclusion point, not wedged in awkwardly

## 8. Platform Compliance (Must Pass)

- [ ] Character limits respected (LinkedIn: 3,000 chars for posts; X: 280 chars per tweet)
- [ ] Formatting is correct for the platform (line breaks, hashtags, mentions)
- [ ] No policy violations (spam, misleading content, prohibited claims)
- [ ] Media assets meet platform specifications (image sizes, video lengths, file formats)
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant and within platform norms (3-5 for LinkedIn, 0-2 for X)

## 9. SEO Compliance (For Search-Targeted Content Only)

- [ ] Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, meta description, and URL slug
- [ ] Title tag is 50-60 characters
- [ ] Meta description is 155-160 characters
- [ ] Heading hierarchy is logical (H1 > H2 > H3, no skipped levels)
- [ ] Internal links are present with keyword-rich anchor text
- [ ] Images have descriptive alt text
- [ ] Content matches the search intent for the target keyword

## 10. Engagement Potential (Should Pass)

- [ ] Would you bookmark this content? (the highest signal of value)
- [ ] Would you share this with a colleague? (organic distribution test)
- [ ] Does this content say something that hasn't been said 100 times before?
- [ ] Is there a specific, memorable takeaway? (if someone reads this, what do they remember tomorrow?)
- [ ] Does the content reward the reader's time? (did they learn something, feel something, or gain something?)

## 11. Visual Direction (Should Pass)

- [ ] Visual notes are included for Design Studio (if visuals are needed)
- [ ] Visual direction is specific enough for Design Studio to execute without guessing
- [ ] Media format and dimensions are specified
- [ ] Any prompt engineer briefs are attached for AI-generated media

## 12. Final Sanity Checks (Should Pass)

- [ ] Spell-check passed (including proper nouns and brand names)
- [ ] No broken links or placeholder URLs
- [ ] Dates and times are correct and timezone-aware
- [ ] Content doesn't accidentally reveal confidential information
- [ ] If content references other people/companies, claims are fair and defensible
- [ ] The content doesn't contradict previously published content

---

## Scoring Guide

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| All "Must Pass" checked | Ready for distribution | Proceed to Content Distributor |
| 1-2 "Must Pass" items failed | Needs minor revision | Return to writer with specific edit notes |
| 3+ "Must Pass" items failed | Needs major revision | Return to writer with structural feedback |
| "Should Pass" items failed | Publishable but suboptimal | Note for future improvement, publish if timeline requires |
