# CTA Library

## What This Is
A reference library of call-to-action templates organized by goal. Every piece of content needs exactly ONE clear CTA. This library helps you pick the right ask for the right situation.

## The One Rule
ONE CTA per piece of content. Not two. Not "DM me or check the link or follow me." Pick ONE action and make it easy to do.

---

## Goal: Drive Engagement (Comments, Replies, Shares)

These CTAs turn passive readers into active participants. Best for building audience relationships and signaling engagement to platform algorithms.

| CTA Template | Example | Best Platform |
|-------------|---------|---------------|
| Ask a specific question | "What's the most expensive AI API call in your product? Drop it in the comments." | LinkedIn, X |
| Ask for their experience | "Have you tried separating deterministic computation from AI in your product? What happened?" | LinkedIn |
| Invite disagreement | "Tell me where I'm wrong on this. I want to stress-test the argument." | LinkedIn, X |
| Ask for a number | "What percentage of your AI product's computation actually uses AI? Reply with your number." | X, LinkedIn |
| Request a tag | "Tag someone building an AI product who needs to see this architecture." | LinkedIn |
| Prompt a bookmark | "Save this thread for the next time you're reviewing your AI cost structure." | X |
| Ask a "would you rather" | "Would you rather have 10x more users or 10x lower cost per user? Reply with your choice." | X, LinkedIn |

## Goal: Drive Traffic (Clicks to Blog, Landing Page, Resource)

These CTAs move people from social platforms to owned properties. Best for growing email lists, blog readership, and product awareness.

| CTA Template | Example | Best Platform |
|-------------|---------|---------------|
| Tease the full breakdown | "I wrote the complete architecture breakdown with code patterns and cost math. Link in comments." | LinkedIn |
| Offer a resource | "I put together a 1-page framework for auditing your AI API calls. DM 'audit' and I'll send it over." | LinkedIn, X |
| Link with a value promise | "Full technical deep-dive on the blog: [link]. Includes the exact caching strategy and configuration." | X, LinkedIn |
| Create a value gap | "This post covers the 'why.' The blog post covers the 'how' — with implementation code: [link]" | LinkedIn |
| Offer the tool | "We open-sourced the cost calculator we used. Plug in your numbers: [link]" | X, LinkedIn, Blog |
| Promise specifics | "The blog post includes the Redis configuration, feature-hashing code, and cache hit rate benchmarks: [link]" | LinkedIn, X |

## Goal: Generate Leads (DMs, Replies, Inquiries)

These CTAs turn interested readers into conversations. Best for consulting, services, and high-ticket products where the sale starts with a conversation.

| CTA Template | Example | Best Platform |
|-------------|---------|---------------|
| Offer a free review | "Building an AI product? DM me — I do free 30-minute architecture reviews." | LinkedIn, X |
| DM with a keyword | "DM me 'architecture' and I'll send you the full framework doc." | LinkedIn |
| Ask if they relate | "If your AI costs look anything like the 'before' numbers, let's talk. Reply or DM." | LinkedIn, Email |
| Offer personalized insight | "I looked at 12 AI products this month. I can usually spot the 3 biggest cost leaks in 15 minutes. DM if useful." | LinkedIn |
| Low-commitment question | "What are you building? Genuinely curious — reply and I'll share any relevant architecture patterns." | X, LinkedIn |
| Social proof + offer | "I've done this review for 15 founders this quarter. Average cost reduction identified: 40x. DM if you want one." | LinkedIn |
| "Reply 'yes' if..." | "Reply 'interested' if you want me to send over a 1-page breakdown of how this applies to your stack." | Email |

## Goal: Drive Conversions (Sign-ups, Purchases, Bookings)

These CTAs ask for a commitment. Best for product launches, waitlist building, and direct sales. Only use after establishing trust through value-first content.

| CTA Template | Example | Best Platform |
|-------------|---------|---------------|
| Waitlist | "AstroAI launches in March. Join the waitlist for early access: [link]" | Email, LinkedIn, X |
| Free trial | "Try the first reading free. No credit card. See the architecture in action: [link]" | Blog, Email |
| Booking link | "Ready to redesign your AI architecture? Grab 30 minutes on my calendar: [link]" | Email, LinkedIn |
| Limited availability | "I'm taking 5 architecture review clients this month. 3 spots left. Book here: [link]" | Email, LinkedIn |
| Risk reversal | "Try it for 14 days. If your cost per user doesn't drop by at least 5x, I'll refund every penny." | Email, Landing page |

## CTA Selection Guide

| Audience Temperature | Best CTA Goal | Logic |
|---------------------|---------------|-------|
| Cold (never heard of you) | Engagement or Traffic | Too early to ask for DMs or purchases. Earn attention first. |
| Warm (follow you, read your content) | Lead Generation or Traffic | They know you — offer something valuable in exchange for conversation. |
| Hot (have interacted, DMed, or visited site) | Conversion or Lead Generation | They're interested — give them a clear path to the next step. |

## CTA Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | Fix |
|-------------|-------------|-----|
| Multiple CTAs | Reader can't decide, does nothing | Pick ONE. The most important one. |
| Vague CTA | "Check it out" — check what, where, why? | Be specific: "Read the full breakdown: [link]" |
| CTA mismatched to content | Asking cold readers to book a call | Match CTA to audience temperature |
| No CTA at all | Great content with no next step — wasted opportunity | Every piece of content gets a CTA |
| Desperate CTA | "PLEASE subscribe and share and retweet!" | Confidence: "Follow for more." Let the content earn it. |
| CTA buried in the middle | Readers often skip to the end or stop before the middle | Place CTA at the natural conclusion point |
