# Hook Formulas

## What This Is
A library of proven hook formulas for opening any piece of content. Hooks are the first 1-2 lines of a post, the first 8 seconds of a video, or the subject line of an email. They decide whether your audience keeps consuming or scrolls away.

## The Rule
The hook has ONE job: earn the next 10 seconds of attention. It doesn't need to summarize the content. It doesn't need to be clever. It needs to make the reader think "wait, what?" or "that's exactly my problem" or "I need to know how."

---

## Formula 1: Contrarian

**Pattern:** Challenge a widely held belief with a bold statement.

**Structure:** "Everyone says {COMMON BELIEF}. {THEY'RE WRONG / HERE'S WHY THAT'S BACKWARDS}."

**Examples:**
- "Everyone says AI is the future. But the best AI products are 95% not-AI."
- "The startup world worships speed. But moving fast is why most AI products fail at scale."
- "Your investors want you to grow at all costs. That advice will bankrupt your AI company."

**Best for:** LinkedIn thought leadership, X threads, blog openings, YouTube cold opens

---

## Formula 2: Stat-Led

**Pattern:** Open with a surprising, specific number that demands explanation.

**Structure:** "{IMPRESSIVE NUMBER}. {CONTEXT THAT MAKES IT MEANINGFUL}."

**Examples:**
- "50,000 calculations per reading. Zero AI API calls."
- "$536,000. That's how much we save per year by NOT using AI for 95% of our computation."
- "97% of AI startups will spend more on API calls than they earn in revenue this year."

**Best for:** LinkedIn posts, X tweets, YouTube thumbnails, email subject lines

---

## Formula 3: Story Open

**Pattern:** Drop the reader into a specific moment — a scene, a conversation, a decision.

**Structure:** "{TIME/PLACE MARKER}, {SPECIFIC THING HAPPENED}."

**Examples:**
- "Last Tuesday, I looked at our API bill and almost cancelled the product launch."
- "Three months into building AstroAI, we realized 95% of our GPT-4 calls were unnecessary."
- "The investor looked at our architecture diagram and said, 'This is the first AI pitch that actually makes financial sense.'"

**Best for:** LinkedIn posts, blog openings, YouTube cold opens, case studies

---

## Formula 4: Result-First

**Pattern:** Lead with the outcome, then promise to explain how.

**Structure:** "{IMPRESSIVE RESULT}. Here's {HOW / THE FRAMEWORK / WHAT WE DID}."

**Examples:**
- "We reduced per-user cost from $0.15 to $0.003. Here's the architecture."
- "Our AI app handles 100,000 daily users with a $30 daily compute bill. Here's the framework."
- "We went from idea to $10K MRR in 6 weeks. Here's the system."

**Best for:** X threads, LinkedIn posts, blog posts, YouTube titles

---

## Formula 5: Question Hook

**Pattern:** Ask a question the audience has been thinking but hasn't articulated.

**Structure:** "{QUESTION THAT HITS A NERVE}?"

**Examples:**
- "What percentage of your AI product's computation actually needs AI?"
- "If your competitor copied your app in 2 weeks, what would you have left?"
- "Are you building an AI product or an API wrapper with a subscription page?"

**Best for:** LinkedIn posts, X tweets, email subject lines, YouTube cold opens

---

## Formula 6: Pattern Interrupt

**Pattern:** Say something unexpected that breaks the reader's autopilot scrolling.

**Structure:** "{UNEXPECTED STATEMENT THAT DOESN'T FIT THE CONTEXT}."

**Examples:**
- "Our astrology app does 50,000 calculations per reading. Zero AI API calls." (astrology + enterprise-grade architecture = unexpected)
- "I charge $300/hour for consulting. Here's the exact framework I use, for free."
- "We deleted 95% of our AI code. Revenue went up."

**Best for:** LinkedIn, X, YouTube thumbnails, anywhere you need scroll-stopping power

---

## Formula 7: How-To Promise

**Pattern:** Promise a specific, actionable skill the reader will have after consuming the content.

**Structure:** "How to {ACHIEVE SPECIFIC OUTCOME} {WITHOUT COMMON OBSTACLE / IN SPECIFIC TIMEFRAME}."

**Examples:**
- "How to reduce your AI API costs by 50x without sacrificing output quality."
- "How to build an AI product that gets cheaper per user as you scale."
- "How to write LinkedIn posts that generate DMs, not just likes."

**Best for:** Blog titles, YouTube titles, email subject lines, tutorial content

---

## Formula 8: Before/After

**Pattern:** Show the transformation from a painful "before" to an attractive "after."

**Structure:** "Before: {PAINFUL STATE}. After: {DESIRABLE STATE}."

**Examples:**
- "Before: $1,500/day in API costs, praying for fewer users. After: $30/day, scaling confidently."
- "Before: 8 seconds to generate a reading. After: 1.4 seconds. Same quality."
- "Before: every competitor could copy us in a week. After: technical moat nobody can replicate without a PhD."

**Best for:** LinkedIn posts, case studies, email sequences, landing pages

---

## Formula 9: Confession / Vulnerability

**Pattern:** Admit a mistake, limitation, or learning — vulnerability earns trust.

**Structure:** "I {MESSED UP / DIDN'T KNOW / WAS WRONG ABOUT} {SPECIFIC THING}. {HERE'S WHAT I LEARNED}."

**Examples:**
- "I spent $4,200 on GPT-4 API calls in one month before realizing 95% of them were unnecessary."
- "We launched our AI product with the worst possible architecture. Here's how we fixed it."
- "For six months, I thought 'AI-powered' meant 'send everything to GPT-4.' I was wrong."

**Best for:** LinkedIn posts, YouTube videos, blog openings, newsletter intros

---

## Formula 10: List of Value

**Pattern:** Promise a specific number of insights, lessons, or tips.

**Structure:** "{NUMBER} {THINGS/LESSONS/MISTAKES/INSIGHTS} about {TOPIC} {OPTIONAL: after EXPERIENCE}."

**Examples:**
- "7 architecture decisions that saved us $536,000 in AI costs."
- "5 mistakes every AI startup makes with their first product (I made all of them)."
- "12 things I learned building 21 AI products in one framework."

**Best for:** X threads, LinkedIn posts, blog posts, YouTube titles

---

## Formula 11: Curiosity Gap

**Pattern:** Create a gap between what the reader knows and what they want to know.

**Structure:** "There's a {ADJECTIVE} reason why {THING} works / doesn't work. {IT'S NOT WHAT YOU THINK}."

**Examples:**
- "There's a counterintuitive reason why our AI app is 50x cheaper. It's not better AI."
- "The most profitable AI companies have something in common. It's not what you'd guess."
- "Our astrology app's biggest competitive advantage has nothing to do with astrology."

**Best for:** LinkedIn, X, YouTube titles, email subject lines

---

## Formula 12: Direct Address

**Pattern:** Call out a specific audience directly — make them feel seen.

**Structure:** "If you're {SPECIFIC SITUATION}, {THIS IS FOR YOU}."

**Examples:**
- "If your AI startup's API bill keeps you up at night, read this."
- "If you're a CTO wondering whether to build or buy your AI layer, I have data."
- "If your AI product works great at 100 users but terrifies you at 100,000, here's your architecture fix."

**Best for:** LinkedIn, email subject lines, YouTube cold opens, ad copy

---

## Formula 13: Myth-Busting

**Pattern:** Name a common myth and declare it false.

**Structure:** "Myth: {COMMON BELIEF}. Reality: {ACTUAL TRUTH}."

**Examples:**
- "Myth: You need AI for everything in an AI product. Reality: The best AI products are 95% deterministic computation."
- "Myth: More features = more value. Reality: One feature done exceptionally beats ten done adequately."
- "Myth: AI products are expensive to build. Reality: The expensive part is the architecture mistakes, not the AI itself."

**Best for:** LinkedIn, blog posts, carousels, X threads

---

## Formula 14: Social Proof

**Pattern:** Lead with evidence that others have already validated this.

**Structure:** "{PROOF OF VALIDATION}. {CONTEXT}."

**Examples:**
- "3 CTOs messaged me this week asking about our architecture after seeing the cost numbers."
- "This framework has been applied to 21 products. The average cost reduction is 40x."
- "After sharing our architecture, 12 founders asked for the same review. Here's what they all had in common."

**Best for:** LinkedIn, email follow-ups, case study intros, landing pages

---

## Formula 15: Prediction

**Pattern:** Make a forward-looking claim about where things are headed.

**Structure:** "In {TIMEFRAME}, {PREDICTION}. Here's why / Here's how to prepare."

**Examples:**
- "In 12 months, every AI SaaS that's still running a pure-wrapper architecture will be acquired or dead."
- "By 2027, the winning AI products won't be the ones with the best models. They'll be the ones with the best caching layers."
- "The next wave of AI startups won't compete on intelligence. They'll compete on efficiency."

**Best for:** LinkedIn thought leadership, X threads, blog posts, keynote scripts

---

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Match the hook to the platform.** Stat-led works everywhere. Story opens work on LinkedIn and YouTube but not in tweets.
2. **Match the hook to the content structure.** Contrarian hooks work best with Hot Take > Evidence > Nuance structure. Result-first works best with Problem > Solution > Evidence.
3. **Write 3 hooks for every piece of content.** Pick the strongest. The other 2 might work as alternative posts later.
4. **The hook must be TRUE.** Bold is good. Exaggerated is bad. If the hook promises something the content doesn't deliver, you've lost trust.
5. **Test hooks on yourself.** If you wouldn't stop scrolling for it, neither will your audience.
