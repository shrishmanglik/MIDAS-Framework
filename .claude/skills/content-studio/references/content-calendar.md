# Content Calendar

## What This Is
A weekly content schedule template and platform-specific posting frequency guide. Used by the Content Strategist for planning and the Content Distributor for scheduling. This is a framework — adjust based on capacity and results.

## Platform-Specific Posting Frequency

### LinkedIn

| Metric | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| **Minimum viable frequency** | 3 posts per week |
| **Optimal frequency** | 5 posts per week (weekdays) |
| **Maximum before fatigue** | 2 posts per day (different types) |
| **Content mix** | 40% insight/opinion, 30% story/case study, 20% tactical/how-to, 10% engagement/community |
| **Article frequency** | 1-2 per month (articles are supplementary, feed posts are primary) |
| **Carousel frequency** | 1-2 per week (high engagement format) |

### X / Twitter

| Metric | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| **Minimum viable frequency** | 1 tweet per day |
| **Optimal frequency** | 3-5 tweets per day (mix of original and engagement) |
| **Thread frequency** | 1-2 per week |
| **Maximum before fatigue** | 10 tweets per day (including replies and quote tweets) |
| **Content mix** | 30% original insights, 20% threads, 20% engagement (replies/quotes), 20% repurposed, 10% personal |

### Blog

| Metric | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| **Minimum viable frequency** | 2 posts per month |
| **Optimal frequency** | 1 post per week |
| **Maximum before quality drops** | 3 posts per week (if resources allow) |
| **Content mix** | 40% evergreen SEO content, 30% thought leadership, 20% tutorials/how-to, 10% case studies |
| **Content refresh cycle** | Review top posts every 90 days, refresh if rankings drop or data outdates |

### Newsletter

| Metric | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| **Frequency** | Weekly (same day and time every week) |
| **Best days** | Tuesday or Thursday morning |
| **Length** | 400-800 words (respect inbox time) |
| **Content mix** | 1 featured piece + 3 quick hits + 3 resources + 1 personal note |
| **Skip policy** | Better to skip a week than send something low-quality |

### YouTube

| Metric | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| **Minimum viable frequency** | 2 videos per month |
| **Optimal frequency** | 1 video per week |
| **Shorts frequency** | 2-3 per week (repurposed from long-form) |
| **Content mix** | 50% educational/tutorial, 30% behind-the-scenes/build-in-public, 20% commentary/opinion |
| **Publishing day** | Same day each week for subscriber habit formation |

## Calendar Management Rules

1. **Plan 2 weeks ahead.** Always have the next 2 weeks fully planned with briefs.
2. **Batch creation.** Create content in batches (all LinkedIn posts for the week in one session), not one piece at a time.
3. **Buffer stock.** Maintain 5-7 days of pre-written content as a buffer for unexpected delays.
4. **Review every Friday.** Review the past week's performance and adjust the next week's plan based on what worked.
5. **Seasonal awareness.** Adjust content around holidays, industry events, product launches, and seasonal trends.
6. **Quality over frequency.** If you can't maintain quality at the planned frequency, reduce frequency. Never reduce quality.
