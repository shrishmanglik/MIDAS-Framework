# Brand Refresh Pipeline

## Overview
Evolution of brand elements while maintaining equity

## Trigger
Activated when task matches this workflow's domain.

## Prerequisites
- Studio SKILL.md loaded
- Required inputs available from prior phase or human

## Phase 1: Audit Current Brand
Assess what works and what needs evolution.

## Phase 2: Strategy
### Agent: brand-strategist (Inline)
Define refresh objectives and boundaries.

## Phase 3: Evolve
Update elements while preserving brand equity.

## Phase 4: Validate
### Agent: brand-voice-guardian (ALWAYS Subagent)
Verify refresh maintains brand recognition.

## Error Recovery
- Phase failure: retry once with enhanced context, then escalate to department head
- Budget exceeded: halt and report to department head
