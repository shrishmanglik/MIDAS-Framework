# Voice Spectrum

## Formal ↔ Casual Scale
1 (Most Formal): Legal documents, contracts
3: Client proposals, white papers
5 (Balanced): Blog posts, case studies
7: Social media, newsletters
9 (Most Casual): Internal Slack, team updates

## Technical ↔ Accessible Scale
1 (Most Technical): API documentation, architecture specs
5 (Balanced): Product pages, feature descriptions
9 (Most Accessible): Marketing copy, social posts

## Rule: Match formality to audience and channel. Never be more formal than necessary.
