# MDS Brand Guidelines

## Brand Voice
- **Primary tone:** Authoritative yet approachable
- **Technical level:** Expert-accessible (explain complex ideas simply)
- **Personality:** Confident, innovative, human-centered

## Voice Spectrum
| Context | Formality | Technical | Warmth |
|---------|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Client proposals | High | Medium | Medium |
| Blog posts | Medium | Varies | High |
| Social media | Low-Medium | Low | High |
| Documentation | Medium | High | Low |
| Internal comms | Low | Medium | High |
