# Pitch Deck Outline Template

> Use this template to structure pitch decks for any audience. Every slide must advance the narrative. If a slide does not answer "why should I care?" for the audience, cut it.

---

## Instructions

1. Select the deck structure that matches your audience and purpose
2. Replace all `[BRACKETED]` placeholders with your specific content
3. Keep slide copy to 5-7 words per bullet, maximum 5 bullets per slide
4. Write speaker notes for every slide (3-5 sentences each)
5. Total deck should not exceed 15 content slides (appendix slides are additional)
6. Allocate 1-2 minutes per slide; reserve 30% of total time for Q&A

## Deck Structures by Audience

### Structure A: Client Sales Deck (Recommended for B2B sales)

| Slide # | Title | Type | Purpose | Time |
|---------|-------|------|---------|------|
| 1 | [Hook / Question] | Hook | Capture attention with a provocative question or statement | 1 min |
| 2 | [The Problem] | Problem | Define the pain the client feels today | 2 min |
| 3 | [The Cost of Inaction] | Problem (data) | Quantify what the problem costs them | 1 min |
| 4 | [Our Solution] | Solution | Show what we do and why it solves the problem | 2 min |
| 5 | [How It Works] | Solution (detail) | Explain the mechanism — simple and clear | 2 min |
| 6 | [The Experience] | Demo / Mockup | Show what the client's users will see | 2 min |
| 7 | [Proof It Works] | Social Proof | Case study, testimonial, or data from existing usage | 2 min |
| 8 | [Technical Foundation] | Technical | Architecture, reliability, accuracy (for technical buyers) | 1 min |
| 9 | [Implementation Plan] | Timeline | How we get from here to live in X weeks | 1 min |
| 10 | [Investment & ROI] | Pricing | What it costs and what they get back | 2 min |
| 11 | [Why Us] | Differentiation | Why we are the best choice for this specific client | 1 min |
| 12 | [Next Steps] | CTA | Clear actions to move forward | 1 min |
| 13 | [Closing Thought] | Emotional close | End with a memorable statement that reinforces the vision | 1 min |

**Total**: ~19 minutes + 11 minutes Q&A = 30-minute meeting

## Slide Template

For each slide, complete this structure:

```markdown
### Slide [N]: [Title]

**Type**: [Hook / Problem / Solution / Data / Proof / Technical / Timeline / Pricing / CTA / Emotional Close]

**Visual**: [Describe what the audience sees — layout, charts, images, mockups, diagrams. Be specific enough for a designer to execute.]

**Copy**:
- [Bullet 1 — 5-7 words maximum]
- [Bullet 2 — 5-7 words maximum]
- [Bullet 3 — 5-7 words maximum]
- [Bullet 4 — 5-7 words maximum (optional)]
- [Bullet 5 — 5-7 words maximum (optional)]

**Speaker Notes**:
> [What to say during this slide. 3-5 sentences. This is the actual presentation — the slide is just the visual anchor. Write conversationally, not formally. Include pauses, emphasis cues, and audience engagement prompts.]

**Transition**:
> [One sentence that bridges to the next slide. Transitions make the narrative feel inevitable.]

**Time**: [X minutes]
```

## Visual Layout Reference

| Slide Type | Recommended Layout |
|------------|-------------------|
| Hook | Full-screen dark background, large centered text, no logo |
| Problem | Large statistic or chart on left, 2-3 bullet points on right |
| Solution | Product screenshot or mockup centered, key benefits below |
| Data | Single chart or graph taking 60% of slide, context annotation |
| Proof | Testimonial quote (large text) with photo/logo, metric below |
| Technical | Simple architecture diagram, minimal text, clean arrows |
| Timeline | Horizontal timeline with phases, milestones marked |
| Pricing | Two-column: pricing table on left, ROI calculation on right |
| CTA | Three numbered steps, clean layout, clear action items |
| Emotional close | Dark background, centered gold/accent text, no bullets |
| Team | Headshot grid with name, title, and one-line credential each |
| Market | Concentric circles (TAM > SAM > SOM) with dollar amounts |

## Slide Copy Rules

1. **5-7 words per bullet** — If you can't say it in 7 words, it's not clear enough
2. **Maximum 5 bullets per slide** — Audience stops reading after 5
3. **One idea per slide** — If the slide covers two topics, split it
4. **No complete sentences** — Bullets are fragments, not paragraphs
5. **Numbers over words** — "3x engagement" is better than "significantly more engagement"
6. **Active verbs** — "Drives retention" not "Can be used to drive retention"
7. **Client language** — Use words the audience uses, not industry jargon they won't recognize

---

## Speaker Notes Rules

1. **3-5 sentences per slide** — Enough to fill 1-2 minutes
2. **Conversational tone** — Write how you speak, not how you write
3. **Include pauses** — Mark moments where you should pause for effect: `[Pause.]`
4. **Anticipate questions** — Note likely questions and brief answers in parentheses
5. **Transition sentences** — End every note with a natural bridge to the next slide
6. **Practice test** — Read the notes aloud. If they sound stiff, rewrite them.

## Appendix Slides

Every deck should have 2-5 appendix slides ready for Q&A:

| Appendix Slide | When to Use |
|----------------|-------------|
| Technical Architecture | When the CTO/engineer asks "how does it work under the hood?" |
| Competitive Comparison | When they ask "how are you different from [competitor]?" |
| Detailed Case Study | When they ask "can you give me more detail on that example?" |
| Security & Compliance | When they ask about data privacy, GDPR, SOC 2 |
| Team Bios | When they ask "who will actually work on this?" |
| Detailed Financials | When investors ask for unit economics or projections |
| Product Roadmap | When they ask "what's coming next?" |

## Timing Guide

| Meeting Length | Content Slides | Q&A Time | Slide Pace |
|---------------|---------------|----------|------------|
| 15 minutes | 8-9 slides | 5 min Q&A | 70 sec/slide |
| 20 minutes | 10-12 slides | 7 min Q&A | 75 sec/slide |
| 30 minutes | 12-13 slides | 10 min Q&A | 90 sec/slide |
| 45 minutes | 13-15 slides | 15 min Q&A | 120 sec/slide |
| 60 minutes | 15 slides + deep dives | 20 min Q&A | 160 sec/slide |

**Rule of thumb**: Always reserve 30% of total meeting time for Q&A. A great pitch invites questions — it does not avoid them.
