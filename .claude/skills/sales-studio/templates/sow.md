# Statement of Work (SOW) Template

> Use this template for all Statements of Work. A SOW is a binding scope document — every word matters. Be precise, be explicit, and leave no room for interpretation. This template is for discussion purposes; all SOWs must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before execution.

---

## Instructions

1. Replace all `[BRACKETED]` placeholders with project-specific content
2. Do NOT leave any section blank — if a section does not apply, state "Not applicable to this engagement" with a brief explanation
3. Every deliverable must have acceptance criteria
4. Payment terms must specify exact amounts, dates, and consequences for late payment
5. Scope exclusions are mandatory — what is NOT included is as important as what IS included
6. Include the legal disclaimer prominently

## 6. Confidentiality

### 6.1 Definition

"Confidential Information" means all non-public business information, technical details, pricing, trade secrets, and user data shared between the parties during this engagement.

### 6.2 Obligations

Both parties agree to:
- Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential
- Use Confidential Information only for the purposes of this SOW
- Not disclose Confidential Information to third parties without prior written consent
- Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used for their own confidential information

### 6.3 Exceptions

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving party
- Was already known to the receiving party prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to the disclosing party)

### 6.4 Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this SOW for a period of [2] years.

## 9. Termination

### 9.1 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this SOW with [14/30] calendar days' written notice. Upon termination for convenience:
- Client pays for all work completed through the date of termination
- Provider delivers all completed work product to Client
- [Any prepaid services continue through the prepaid period / are prorated and refunded]

### 9.2 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this SOW immediately upon written notice if the other party:
- Materially breaches this SOW and fails to cure the breach within [14] calendar days of written notice specifying the breach
- Becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases business operations

### 9.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:
1. Provider will deliver all completed work and work-in-progress to Client within [5] business days
2. Client will pay all outstanding invoices within [15] business days
3. Sections that survive termination: Confidentiality, IP, Limitation of Liability, and any accrued payment obligations

## SOW Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any SOW, verify:

- [ ] Legal disclaimer is prominently displayed at top and bottom
- [ ] Both parties are clearly identified with contact information
- [ ] Scope includes explicit inclusions AND exclusions
- [ ] Every deliverable has a due date and acceptance criteria
- [ ] Payment amounts, schedule, and net terms are specific (no "TBD")
- [ ] Late payment consequences are defined
- [ ] Intellectual property ownership is clearly assigned
- [ ] Confidentiality terms are included with a defined survival period
- [ ] Warranties are specific about what IS and IS NOT guaranteed
- [ ] Liability is capped at a reasonable amount for both parties
- [ ] Termination clause exists for both convenience and cause
- [ ] Change request procedure is defined
- [ ] Governing law jurisdiction is specified (or flagged for legal review)
- [ ] Dispute resolution mechanism is specified
- [ ] Force majeure clause is included
- [ ] Signature block is present for both parties
- [ ] Document is internally consistent (dates, amounts, names match throughout)
- [ ] Language is clear and plain (minimal legal jargon)
