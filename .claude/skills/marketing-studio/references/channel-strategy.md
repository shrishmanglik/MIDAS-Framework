# Channel Strategy Reference

Guide for selecting and prioritizing marketing channels based on audience, budget, and business stage.

---

## Channel Selection Framework

### Step 1: Where does the audience congregate?

Map your target audience to the channels where they already spend time and attention.

| Audience Type | Primary Channels | Secondary Channels |
|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Technical professionals | Google Search, GitHub, Stack Overflow, Dev Twitter, Reddit | LinkedIn, Hacker News, YouTube |
| Young consumers (18-30) | Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit | Snapchat, Twitter, Google Search |
| Business professionals | LinkedIn, Google Search, Email | Twitter, YouTube, Industry events |
| Indian diaspora (25-40) | Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, Google Search | Reddit, Facebook, Twitter |
| Small business owners | Google Search, Facebook, Email | Instagram, YouTube, Local SEO |

### Step 2: Match channel to funnel stage

| Funnel Stage | Best Channels | Goal |
|-------------|--------------|------|
| **Awareness** | Content/SEO, Social media, Display ads, PR, Influencers | Get on the radar |
| **Consideration** | Google Search, Retargeting, Email nurture, Comparison content | Be evaluated |
| **Conversion** | Landing pages, Email sequences, Retargeting, Direct outreach | Close the deal |
| **Retention** | Email, In-app messaging, Community, Content | Keep them engaged |
| **Referral** | Referral programs, Social sharing, Community | Grow through users |

### Step 3: Match channel to budget

| Budget Range (Monthly) | Recommended Channels |
|----------------------|---------------------|
| $0 (bootstrapped) | SEO/Content, Social media (organic), Community, Email |
| $500-2,000 | Above + Google Ads (targeted), Meta Ads (small tests) |
| $2,000-10,000 | Above + Broader paid ads, Influencer partnerships, Retargeting |
| $10,000+ | Above + Multi-channel paid, Sponsorships, Events, PR |

## Channel Profiles

### SEO / Content Marketing

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | Long-term sustainable traffic, building authority |
| **Time to Results** | 3-6 months for meaningful organic traffic |
| **Cost** | Low monetary cost, high time investment |
| **Scalability** | Excellent — compounds over time |
| **Measurability** | High (Google Search Console, Analytics) |
| **Key Metrics** | Organic traffic, keyword rankings, organic signups |
| **When to Use** | Always — start early, results come later |
| **When NOT to Use** | When you need results in < 30 days |

### Google Search Ads

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | Capturing existing demand (people searching for a solution) |
| **Time to Results** | Days — almost immediate |
| **Cost** | Medium-high ($1-15 CPC depending on competition) |
| **Scalability** | Limited by search volume |
| **Measurability** | Excellent (direct intent → conversion tracking) |
| **Key Metrics** | CPC, CTR, CPA, ROAS |
| **When to Use** | When people are actively searching for your category |
| **When NOT to Use** | When nobody searches for your category yet |

### Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | Creating demand, reaching specific demographics/interests |
| **Time to Results** | Days to weeks (learning phase) |
| **Cost** | Low-medium ($0.50-5 CPC, varies by audience) |
| **Scalability** | Excellent — massive reach |
| **Measurability** | Good (pixel tracking, but iOS privacy impacts attribution) |
| **Key Metrics** | CPA, CTR, ROAS, frequency |
| **When to Use** | When you can target by interests/demographics; visual product |
| **When NOT to Use** | B2B enterprise; highly technical audiences |

### LinkedIn Ads

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | B2B targeting by job title, company size, industry |
| **Time to Results** | Weeks |
| **Cost** | High ($5-15 CPC, $30+ CPM) |
| **Scalability** | Limited by audience size |
| **Measurability** | Good for B2B pipeline tracking |
| **Key Metrics** | CPC, CTR, lead quality, pipeline generated |
| **When to Use** | B2B products targeting specific roles/companies |
| **When NOT to Use** | Consumer products; budget under $2,000/month |

### Email Marketing

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | Nurturing leads, onboarding, retention, conversions |
| **Time to Results** | Immediate (for existing list) |
| **Cost** | Very low ($20-100/month for tool + time) |
| **Scalability** | Scales with list size |
| **Measurability** | Excellent (open, click, conversion tracking) |
| **Key Metrics** | Open rate, click rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate |
| **When to Use** | Always — from day 1 |
| **When NOT to Use** | You have no email list and no way to build one |

### Reddit / Community Marketing

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | Niche audiences, authenticity-driven communities |
| **Time to Results** | Weeks to months (trust takes time) |
| **Cost** | Free (organic) to low (ads are cheap) |
| **Scalability** | Limited by community size |
| **Measurability** | Low-medium (hard to track attribution) |
| **Key Metrics** | Upvotes, comments, referral traffic, signups |
| **When to Use** | Niche product with a dedicated subreddit/community |
| **When NOT to Use** | Mass-market product; if you're unwilling to genuinely participate |

### Influencer Marketing

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Best For** | Reaching engaged audiences through trusted voices |
| **Time to Results** | Days to weeks (after campaign goes live) |
| **Cost** | Variable ($50 for micro-influencers to $10K+ for major ones) |
| **Scalability** | Medium — depends on finding the right influencers |
| **Measurability** | Medium (use unique promo codes or UTM links) |
| **Key Metrics** | Impressions, engagement, signups via tracked links/codes |
| **When to Use** | Consumer product in a passionate niche |
| **When NOT to Use** | B2B enterprise; no relevant influencers in the space |

## Budget Allocation Guidelines

### For a $1,000/month SaaS Launch

| Channel | Allocation | Rationale |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Content/SEO | $0 (time investment) | Long-term foundation; 4 articles/month |
| Meta Ads | $500 (50%) | Primary paid acquisition channel |
| Google Search Ads | $200 (20%) | Capture existing search demand |
| Community/Reddit | $0 (time investment) | Organic community participation |
| Experimentation | $200 (20%) | Test one new channel per month |
| Email tooling | $100 (10%) | Email platform subscription |

### Reallocation Triggers

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Channel CPA exceeds 2x target | Reduce budget by 50%, reallocate to best performer |
| Channel CPA is under 50% target | Increase budget by 50% to scale winner |
| No conversions after $200 spent | Pause channel, diagnose (targeting? creative? landing page?) |
| Organic traffic growing > 20% MoM | Content is working — double down on SEO content production |
